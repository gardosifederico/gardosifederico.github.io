Photo Display

The Home NAS stores in the Home network all Photos collected, and an old Ipad from 2013 was waiting to be used.

Design Objectives

- The System shall disply on a dedicated website, internal to the home network, images from the NAS.
- The System shall be accessible outside of the home LAN, without using Port Forwarding.
- The photos displayed shall not be duplicated
- The system shall not use databases

HW and SW

- Pi 4 (used for Home NAS)
- Photo visualizer device (Ipad in my case)
Besides nginx no dedicated SW has been used.
Every first day of each quarter, a catalog of all images available in the NAS is generated, in the form of a JSON file. The catalog contains the filepath, album name and date. The catalog.json file is used by nginx for displaying the picture with an image change every 30s.
The catalog is deployed every quarter after the generation of the updated catalog, updating the catalog with the new iamges stored on the NAS, updating the images to be visualized.
